COMMISSION
& CANCELLATION.

The Partner Kit explains how commission is earned. This document explains what happens to commission when a booking changes, shrinks or cancels.

COMMISSION IS PAID AT THE
NON-REFUNDABLE POINT.

For the current program, that point is one week before departure, when the balance is due in full and the booking is no longer refundable under the standard customer policy.

Internal partner document · Not for public distribution
C01 / What Is Paid

CONFIRMED
TRAVELLERS.

Commission is paid on confirmed travellers only. Leads, enquiries and quotations do not qualify.

A traveller becomes confirmed when the required advance has been received and written confirmation has been issued.

ELIGIBLE BOOKING VALUE
Package value received by The Himalayan Ramble, excluding applicable taxes/GST and third-party costs booked at cost. Ground extras and chargeable items do not earn commission.

C03 / Tier Jump

THE TIER
MOVES UP.

  1. 01You bring 10 confirmed travellers. They fall under the 4% tier.
  2. 02Commission is calculated on the eligible booking value of those 10.
  3. 03Later in the same month, another 10 travellers confirm. Your total is now 20.
  4. 0420 travellers fall under the 6% tier. All 20 are recalculated at 6%.
  5. 05Commission already paid at 4% is deducted. Only the remaining balance is paid.
THE HIGHER TIER APPLIES TO THE TOTAL ELIGIBLE BUSINESS
FOR THAT MONTH.
C04 / Slot Standard

FILL
THE SLOT.

There is no minimum number of confirmed travellers per month. Each fixed departure is a separate slot, and partners are expected to work toward filling the relevant slot.

Current Manali Standard10–12+ confirmed travellers per departure slot.
TargetNo later than 30 days before the specific departure.
MeasuredSlot by slot, not month by month.
Confirmed MeansBooked and paid through the required process, not merely enquired or pencilled in.

A slot is considered filled when it reaches the required group size for that product. This is a performance standard, not an automatic cancellation or penalty mechanism.

Commission continues to follow the monthly tier structure independently.

C05 / Three-Month Performance Path

PROVE
PERFORMANCE.
GROW.

The initial partnership period is three months on a commission basis. It is designed to demonstrate that the partner can find customers, build groups, contribute to filling departure slots and convert opportunities into real confirmed travellers.

50+Confirmed travellers across the initial three-month period

The 50+ figure is counted individually across the full three-month period. It is not a monthly minimum.

  • 01Work actively toward filling departure slots.
  • 02Generate more than 50 confirmed travellers across the three-month period.
  • 03Convert genuine group opportunities into real bookings.
  • 04Communicate professionally and follow through efficiently.
  • 05Demonstrate that the same level of performance can continue across future tours.
C06 / Full-Time Opportunity

THE NEXT
STEP.

If a partner successfully demonstrates the required performance and can credibly continue delivering results for future tours with the same consistency and efficiency, The Himalayan Ramble may offer a full-time position with salary.

Three months does not automatically create a full-time position. Any transition depends on performance, consistency, slot-filling ability, confirmed travellers, future potential, business requirements and mutual agreement.
C07 / Payout

WHEN
COMMISSION
IS PAID.

Commission is released when a booking passes its non-refundable point, currently one week before departure. It is not held until the tour is completed.

CalculationUsing the tier for the calendar month in which the travellers were confirmed.
SettlementMonthly, against a statement covering bookings that passed the non-refundable point during that month.
Outstanding BalanceIf the customer's balance has not been received in full, commission is held until it is received.
Transfer CostsBank-transfer and currency-conversion charges are borne by the partner unless otherwise agreed in writing.
C08 / Customer Cancellation

WHEN
THE CUSTOMER
CANCELS.

MORE THAN 20 DAYSNo cancellation charge on tour cost under the standard customer policy. Deposit is refunded after non-refundable components and unrecoverable supplier costs are deducted.
20–10 DAYS20% of package cost retained.
10 DAYS OR LESSNo refund. No-show is treated the same.
DEPOSIT30% of the advance is non-refundable.
CANCELLATION MUST BE SUBMITTED IN WRITING. The date THR receives the notice is the date used to apply the policy.
C09 / Effect On Commission

WHEN THE
BOOKING
SHRINKS.

Cancelled TravellerA cancelled traveller leaves the partner's traveller count for the relevant month.
Retained AmountsNo commission is paid on retained or forfeited amounts. Retained money covers costs already committed to hotels, vehicles and operators. It is not margin.
Before Non-Refundable PointThe traveller simply leaves the count. Nothing has been paid as commission, so nothing is recovered.
Tier ReductionIf a cancellation drops the month into a lower commission tier, the month is recalculated at the lower rate and the difference is adjusted against the next settlement.
After Non-Refundable PointCancellations after the non-refundable point do not affect commission already paid.
C10 / Edge Cases & Records

KEEP THE
NUMBERS CLEAN.

Group ShrinksA group of 12 that travels as 9 earns commission on 9. The 3 cancelled travellers leave the relevant count and may change the tier.
Date ChangeA date change is not a cancellation. The traveller remains credited to the partner and is counted in the month in which they finally travel. Amendment costs do not earn commission.
Partnership EndsCommission remains payable for confirmed bookings that reach the non-refundable point. Nothing further accrues after the partnership ends.
RecordsTHR booking records are the reference for traveller counts, eligible value and commission tier.
Statement QueriesQueries must be raised within 15 days of receiving the statement.
AttributionIf two partners claim the same customer, the earliest recorded introduction stands.
No RebatesCommission may not be discounted, rebated or shared with the customer as an inducement unless expressly authorised in writing.
