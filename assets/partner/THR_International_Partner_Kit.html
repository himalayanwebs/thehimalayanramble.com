01 / welcome & the program

welcome.

Welcome to The Himalayan Ramble International Travel Partner Network.

Founded in 2000 and based in Shimla, India, The Himalayan Ramble specialises in Himalayan travel, group tours, trekking, adventure expeditions and customised travel experiences.

Through this partnership, we connect travellers from your market with authentic and professionally organised Himalayan experiences. Partners may operate from any country or market and represent The Himalayan Ramble within their own network.

25years operating
7regions across northern india

the program

The International Travel Partner Program builds a network of trusted partners who introduce and promote The Himalayan Ramble's travel experiences within their local market.

as a partner, you will:

  1. 01Identify potential customers and groups.
  2. 02Promote our approved tour packages.
  3. 03Pitch the tours to your customer network.
  4. 04Help convert interested prospects into confirmed bookings.
  5. 05Work with The Himalayan Ramble team for quotations, planning and final booking arrangements.

This is a performance-based, part-time international partnership focused on generating consistent group bookings through your own network and market. You handle customer acquisition and relationship management; The Himalayan Ramble handles destination expertise, quotations, travel planning, operations and final booking confirmation.

network equation

your network + our expertise = more travellers.

02 / role, responsibilities & how it works

your role.

Your primary role is to find, approach, pitch and convert customers within your network.

you are responsible for:

  • Answering basic customer questions using the information provided.

commission is paid on confirmed bookings, not on leads.A lead, enquiry or quotation does not qualify. Rates are fixed and non-negotiable. The tier is set by your total confirmed travellers in the calendar month and applied to the total eligible booking value for that month.

how the tier jump pays — worked example

  1. 01You bring 10 confirmed travellers. They fall under the 4% tier.
  2. 02Commission is paid at 4% on the eligible booking value of those 10.
  3. 03Later that month, 10 more confirm. Your month total is now 20.
  4. 0420 travellers fall under the 6% tier. All 20 are recalculated at 6%.
  5. 05The 4% already paid is deducted. Only the remaining balance is paid.
end-of-month position

you end the month on 6% of everything.

08 / fill the slot

fill
the slot.

There is no minimum number of confirmed travellers per month. Instead, each fixed departure date is a slot, and your responsibility is to ensure your slot reaches full group size before it closes.

the deadline

Every departure slot should be filled to the required group size no later than 30 days before that departure date. This applies slot by slot, not month by month — each date carries its own deadline, independent of any other.

what “filled” means

The slot reaches the group size confirmed for that product — currently 10-12+ travellers for the Manali Winter Circuit. Confirmed means booked and paid, not enquired or pencilled in.

why this matters

This is a desired performance condition, not a hard cutoff — nothing automatically happens to a slot that is not filled by the deadline. It is, however, the standard The Himalayan Ramble uses when reviewing partner performance for future roles, renewed terms and additional markets.

Commission itself is unaffected by this condition and continues to follow the monthly tiered structure set out on the previous page.

This applies to all current and future Himalayan Ramble products sold through the partner program.

09 / sales guidelines

do.
do not.

do

  • Be professional and respectful.
  • Use accurate and approved information.
  • Explain the experience clearly.
  • Follow up with interested customers.
  • Be transparent about pricing.
  • Ask questions before making recommendations.
  • Contact The Himalayan Ramble when you need clarification.

do not

  • Promise unauthorised discounts.
  • Change the official package price.
  • Guarantee services that are not confirmed.
  • Promise specific hotels without confirmation.
  • Make visa guarantees.
  • Guarantee weather conditions.
  • Promise refunds outside official policies.
  • Provide inaccurate information.
golden rule

never guess when you can ask.

10 / handling objections

when they
push back.

“why should we visit manali?”

Manali offers a unique combination of Himalayan landscapes, nature, culture and adventure. A great option beyond conventional city or beach holidays.

“us$500 is expensive.”

US$500 is the approximate starting price. The final price depends on itinerary, dates and requirements. The Himalayan Ramble team provides the exact quotation.

“can we travel with only four people?”

The tour is primarily designed for groups of 10-12 or more. Smaller groups may be considered in special circumstances — I can check with The Himalayan Ramble.

“can you give us a discount?”

Pricing is determined by The Himalayan Ramble. I cannot promise a discount, but I can ask the team about the applicable quotation.

“can we customise the trip?”

Yes, customisation may be possible depending on requirements. I can connect you with The Himalayan Ramble's team to discuss your group.

you cannot promise price.
you can promise an answer.
