FROM THE PLAINS TO THE SNOW LINE

PLAINS
TO
SNOW LINE

Eight days from the plains of the Mughal Empire to the snow line. Begin at the Taj Mahal and Agra Fort, travel overnight into the foothills at Shimla, then head north to Manali, the last major town before the route crosses into Lahaul.

Experience open snow at Sissu, fly above Solang Valley, and walk through cedar forest beside the Beas River. Every stage of the route is road-accessible.

ITINERARY04 / 16
DAY 01 / DAY 02

DELHI → AGRA
→ SHIMLA

DAY 01 / DELHI TO AGRA

Morning pickup from Indira Gandhi International Airport. Travel by private vehicle to Agra, check in, rest and spend the evening at leisure. Overnight in Agra.

IGI airport pickup · Private road transfer · Overnight Agra

DAY 02 / AGRA TO SHIMLA

Start early for the Taj Mahal, on foot or by tuk-tuk. Return to the hotel, collect luggage and depart. Visit Agra Fort by vehicle, the red sandstone fortress that served as a centre of Mughal power for more than a century. Leave for Shimla in the evening on an overnight road journey.

Taj Mahal · Agra Fort · Overnight road journey

SHIMLA
TO MANALI

DAY 03 / SHIMLA

Arrive in the early morning, check in and rest. After breakfast, walk up to Jakhu through the cedar-covered ridge or take the cable car. Descend to Mall Road and explore the colonial ridge at your own pace. Overnight in Shimla.

Jakhu Ridge walk · Mall Road · Overnight Shimla

DAY 04 / SHIMLA TO MANALI

Depart after breakfast. Stop for lunch near Bilaspur beside the Beas River. Arrive in Manali in the evening, check in, then walk the Mall Road. Overnight in Manali.

Lunch stop at Bilaspur · Evening Mall Road · Overnight Manali

DAY 05 / DAY 06

ABOVE
3,000 METRES

DAY 05 / ATAL TUNNEL & SISSU

Head north from Manali through the Atal Tunnel, the world's longest highway tunnel at this altitude. Enter Lahaul at Sissu for open snow and valley views. Return to Manali in the evening, walk the Mall Road and overnight in Manali.

Atal Tunnel · Sissu snow experience · Overnight Manali

DAY 06 / SOLANG VALLEY

Spend the day in Solang, one of Himachal's most accessible adventure areas. Paragliding is included. Zipline, skiing, the snow-point cable car, ATV rides and tube slides are available locally at additional cost. Walk Old Manali in the evening. Overnight in Manali.

Paragliding included · Solang Valley · Old Manali

DAY 07 / KOTHI & DEPARTURE

Walk through the Manali forest reserve and cedar woodland in the morning. Continue to Kothi near Palchan for bungee jumping over the valley. Depart for Delhi around 15:00–16:00 on an overnight road journey.

Forest walk · Kothi bungee jump, additional cost · Overnight road journey

DAY 08 / ARRIVE DELHI

Arrive at Delhi IGI Airport in the early morning, approximately 05:00–06:00. Tour ends.

IGI airport arrival · Airport drop assistance · Tour ends

ROUTE & WEATHERThe Atal Tunnel and road to Sissu are weather-dependent and may close without notice. If a section is impassable, we will use the nearest practical alternative at no additional cost. If closure requires an extra night, extra day or full rerouting, costs are charged at actual cost.
SNOW & VEHICLEThe Tempo Traveller handles the standard route well, but heavy snow may make some high-altitude or single-lane sections inaccessible and may cause the vehicle to become stuck or damaged. If route conditions require it, a temporary 4x4 will be arranged for that section at actual cost and is not included.
