## Why travel with us

Sanjay Thakur started The Himalayan Ramble in Shimla in 2000 with one idea: travel through the Himalaya should be hassle-free, not a gamble on an operator who has never seen the pass you are asking about. Twenty-five years on, the job is the same — customized routes, licensed guides and porters who know the terrain and the culture, and a small Sanjauli office that has guided thousands of travelers from India and abroad through Himachal, Ladakh, and Jammu & Kashmir.

- 25 years guiding the Himalaya

## Signature routes

- **Frozen Spiti Valley** — 7 nights / 8 days, November–February. Ice climbing, snowbound monasteries, and snowshoeing through a valley that closes off to most of the world every winter. https://thehimalayanramble.com/himachal-pradesh/spiti/
- **Kinnaur – Spiti – Chandratal – Manali** — 9 nights / 10 days, May–October. Apple orchards to high-altitude desert to a lake that turns silver at dusk. https://thehimalayanramble.com/himachal-pradesh/kinnaur/
- **Shimla – Kullu – Manali** — Custom duration, year-round. The classic Himachal route: colonial hill-station mornings, valley orchards, and alpine passes. https://thehimalayanramble.com/himachal-pradesh/shimla/
- **Zanskar Range Expedition** — Custom duration, July–September. Advanced trekking and Class III–V rafting on the Zanskar River. https://thehimalayanramble.com/ladakh/zanskar/
