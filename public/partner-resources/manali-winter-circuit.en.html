OVERVIEW02 / 16
Plains to the snowline.

PLAINS
TO THE
SNOWLINE.

Eight days from the Mughal plains to the snowline. You begin at the Taj Mahal and Agra Fort, climb overnight into the foothills at Shimla, then push north to Manali, the last town before the road crosses into Lahaul.

You stand in open snow at Sissu, fly over Solang Valley, and walk the deodar forest below the Beas. Every stretch of this route is reached by road.

ITINERARY04 / 16
Day 01 / Day 02

DELHI → AGRA
→ SHIMLA

DAY 01 / DELHI TO AGRA

Morning pickup from Indira Gandhi International Airport. Road transfer to Agra by private vehicle. Check in, rest and settle in for the evening. Overnight Agra.

IGI AIRPORT PICKUP · PRIVATE ROAD TRANSFER · OVERNIGHT AGRA

DAY 02 / AGRA TO SHIMLA

Early start for the Taj Mahal, reached on foot or by tuk-tuk. Return to the hotel, pack and load the vehicle. Agra Fort by vehicle, the red sandstone seat of Mughal power for over a century. Depart for Shimla by evening on an overnight road journey.

TAJ MAHAL · AGRA FORT · OVERNIGHT ROAD JOURNEY

Day 03 / Day 04

SHIMLA

DAY 03 / SHIMLA

Arrive early morning. Hotel check-in, rest and freshen up. After breakfast, a walking tour up to Jakhu through the cedar ridge, on foot or by ropeway. Descend to Mall Road and explore the colonial ridge at your own pace. Overnight Shimla.

JAKHU RIDGE WALK · MALL ROAD · OVERNIGHT SHIMLA

DAY 04 / SHIMLA TO MANALI

Depart after breakfast. Lunch stop near Bilaspur on the Beas. Arrive Manali by evening, check in, then an evening walk along Mall Road. Overnight Manali.

BILASPUR LUNCH STOP · MALL ROAD BY NIGHT · OVERNIGHT MANALI

DAY 05 / ATAL TUNNEL AND SISSU

North from Manali through the Atal Tunnel, the world's longest highway tunnel above 3,000 metres, emerging into Lahaul at Sissu for open snow and the valley view. Return to Manali by evening. Mall Road walk. Overnight Manali.

ATAL TUNNEL · SISSU SNOW POINT · OVERNIGHT MANALI

DAY 06 / SOLANG VALLEY

A full day at Solang, Himachal's most accessible adventure ground. Paragliding is included. Zipline, skiing, cable car to the snow point, ATV and tube slide are available on site at your own cost. Evening walk through Old Manali. Overnight Manali.

PARAGLIDING INCLUDED · SOLANG VALLEY · OLD MANALI

DAY 07 / KOTHI AND DEPARTURE

Morning walk through the Manali forest park and deodar reserve. On to Kothi, near Palchan, for bungee jumping over the gorge. Depart for Delhi around 3 to 4 pm on an overnight road journey.

FOREST PARK WALK · KOTHI BUNGEE, AT OWN COST · OVERNIGHT ROAD JOURNEY

DAY 08 / ARRIVAL DELHI

Reach Delhi IGI Airport in the early morning, approximately 5 to 6 am. End of tour.

IGI AIRPORT ARRIVAL · AIRPORT DROP ASSISTANCE · END OF TOUR

ON
THE
ROAD.

ROUTE & WEATHERThe Atal Tunnel and the Sissu road are weather-dependent and may close without notice. Where a section is not passable we substitute the nearest viable alternative at no extra cost. Where a closure forces an added night, added day or full re-route, that is chargeable at cost.
SNOW & VEHICLEA tempo traveller handles the standard route well, but in heavy snowfall it may be unable to reach certain high-altitude or single-lane sections, and may get stuck or sustain damage. Where conditions require it we arrange a temporary 4x4 transfer for that leg, at cost, not covered by the package.
ALTITUDEThe circuit reaches approximately 3,100 m at Sissu and sleeps at 2,050 m in Manali. Travellers confirm they are fit to travel at altitude. Evacuation and medical costs are to the traveller's account.
ACTIVITIESParagliding and every optional activity at Solang and Kothi run at the operator's discretion, subject to weather, wind and safety clearance on the day. Where the included flight cannot be flown for reasons of weather or safety, no cash refund is issued against it.
ACCOMMODATIONProperties are confirmed by category. Named properties are shared before departure. If a property becomes unavailable, an equivalent in the same category is substituted and you are told before departure.
ITINERARYThe Himalayan Ramble may alter the sequence of the itinerary in the interest of safety, road conditions or guest wellbeing. The overall content of the tour is maintained.
