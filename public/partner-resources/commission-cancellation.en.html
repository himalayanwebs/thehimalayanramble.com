Section 01 / What commission is paid on

Paid on
travellers.

  • Paid on confirmed travellers only. Never on leads, enquiries or quotations.
  • A traveller is confirmed once the advance has been received and written confirmation issued.
  • Calculated on the eligible booking value: the package value received by The Himalayan Ramble, excluding taxes and GST and any third-party cost booked at cost.
  • Extras bought by the customer on the ground carry no commission.

Section 03 / How the tier moves up

Moving up,
getting paid.

  • Reach a higher tier later in the same month and the higher rate applies to the whole month. Commission already paid at the lower tier is deducted, and only the balance is released.
  • Ten travellers paid at 4%, then ten more that month. Twenty sits at 6%, so all twenty are recalculated at 6% and the 4% already paid is deducted.
Section 04 / Performance requirement
50+
confirmed travellers
across three months.

There is no minimum number of confirmed travellers required per calendar month. The previous monthly minimum has been removed. The three-month requirement is measured across the entire initial commission period.

The count is travellers, not bookings. A booking for 12 people counts as 12 confirmed travellers.

Continuation into a full-time role is based on demonstrated performance, consistency and the ability to continue producing bookings for upcoming tours at the same efficiency.
Section 05 / Payout
  • Released once a booking passes its non-refundable point, one week before departure. It is not held until the tour is completed.
  • Calculated at the tier for the month in which those travellers were confirmed.
  • Settled monthly, against a statement covering every booking that passed its non-refundable point that month.
  • Where the customer's balance is not received in full, commission is held until it is.
  • Bank transfer and currency conversion charges are borne by the partner unless agreed in writing.

Section 05 / Slot Filling04 / 09
Performance standard / Scheduled tours

Fill
the slot.

There is no monthly minimum. Instead, each fixed departure date is treated as an individual tour slot, and the partner's responsibility is to work toward filling the available slots before departure.

What counts as filled?

A slot is considered filled when it reaches the required group size for that product. For the current Manali Winter Circuit, this means 10–12+ confirmed travellers.

Confirmed means the traveller has completed the required booking process and payment necessary for the booking to be treated as confirmed. Leads, enquiries, quotations and informal expressions of interest do not count.

Why it matters.

Slot filling is a performance standard used by The Himalayan Ramble to evaluate the partner's ability to generate and convert business. It is not a cancellation penalty and does not automatically cancel, reduce or remove a tour slot when the target is not reached.

It is a measure of performance for continuation of the partnership and consideration of future opportunities.

Commission is separate. The slot-filling standard does not change the monthly commission calculation. Commission continues to be calculated according to the partner's total confirmed travellers in each calendar month and the applicable commission tier.

The initial three-month period is commission-based. It is designed to demonstrate that the partner can consistently find customers, build groups, fill tour slots and convert opportunities into confirmed travellers.

Section 07 / Effect on your commission
  • A cancelled traveller leaves your month's count.
  • No commission is paid on retained or forfeited amounts. Retained money covers cost already committed to hotels, vehicle and operators. It is not margin.
  • A cancellation before the non-refundable point simply removes the traveller. Nothing has been paid, so nothing is recovered.
  • If that drops the month into a lower tier, the month is recalculated at the lower rate and the difference is adjusted against your next settlement.
  • Cancellations after the non-refundable point do not affect commission already paid.

Section 08 / Edge Cases07 / 09
Section 08 / Shrinking groups and date changes

The edge
cases.

  • A group of twelve that travels as nine earns on nine. The three who cancelled leave your count and may move your tier.
  • A date change is not a cancellation. The traveller stays credited to you and counts toward the month they finally travel. Amendment costs carry no commission.
  • If the partnership ends, commission stays payable on confirmed bookings that reach their non-refundable point. Nothing accrues afterwards.
Section 09 / Records and attribution
  • The Himalayan Ramble's booking records are the reference for counts, eligible value and tier.
  • Queries on a statement must be raised within 15 days of receiving it.
  • Where two partners claim the same customer, the earliest recorded introduction stands.
  • Commission may not be discounted, rebated or shared with the customer as an inducement.
