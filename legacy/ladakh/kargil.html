Kargil

On the Srinagar–Leh highway, roughly the halfway point — and the gateway to Zanskar.

Plan Your Kargil Trip

A crossroads town, and a different Ladakh.

Kargil is Ladakh's second-largest town, and it feels distinct from Leh — a Shia Muslim majority rather than the Buddhist culture more visible further east, and a working town rather than a tourist base. Most travelers pass through as the natural overnight stop on the Srinagar–Leh highway, or as the turn-off point for the road into Zanskar.

Nearby Drass, sometimes called the second-coldest inhabited place in the world, holds the Kargil War Memorial, commemorating the 1999 conflict fought in these mountains.

A war memorial, and a quiet valley behind it.

  • 01
    Kargil War Memorial, DrassA memorial to the soldiers of the 1999 Kargil War, set against the peaks where it was fought.
  • 02
    Suru ValleyA quieter valley south of Kargil, with views toward the Nun and Kun peaks.
  • 03
    Kargil–Padum RoadThe overland route into Zanskar, usually broken up with a night in Kargil.

Best time & getting here.

Best Time to Visit

May–October, matching the open season of the Srinagar–Leh and Kargil–Zanskar roads.

Getting Here

Roughly midway on the Srinagar–Leh highway — about equal driving distance from either city.

Activities

War memorial visit, Suru Valley excursions, and staging for the road into Zanskar.

Good To Know

We usually build Kargil in as an overnight stop rather than a standalone destination — on the way to Leh, Zanskar, or back toward Srinagar.
