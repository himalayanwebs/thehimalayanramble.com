The Blog

Field notes from twenty-five years in the mountains.

Organized the way we actually think about the Himalaya — by region, then by place. All seven regions we operate in, one route at a time.

Region 1 of 7

Himachal Pradesh

Our home turf. Shimla is where the office has always been — these five places are where most of our trips actually go.

Shimla

The colonial hill capital, seven hills, pine and deodar forest.

Manali

Base camp for Rohtang, Solang, and the road north to Ladakh.

Spiti

High-altitude cold desert, older than the monsoon it hides from.

Kinnaur

The Sutlej valley, apple orchards climbing into bare rock.

Kullu

Valley of Gods — rafting on the Beas and the Dussehra crowds.

Region 2 of 7

Ladakh

A high-altitude desert on the Tibetan Plateau — monasteries, passes above 5,000m, and a lake that changes colour through the day.

Leh

The old capital, and base for every route into the region.

Nubra Valley

Sand dunes and Bactrian camels, over the Khardung La.

Pangong Lake

A lake stretching two-thirds of the way into Tibet.

Zanskar

Class III–V rafting in summer, the Chadar trek in winter.

Kargil

The Srinagar–Leh halfway point, and gateway to Zanskar.
View All Ladakh → Full region overview

Region 3 of 7

Jammu & Kashmir

Houseboats on Dal Lake, ski slopes at Gulmarg, and the pilgrim road to Vaishno Devi.

Srinagar

Dal Lake houseboats and Mughal gardens.

Gulmarg

Skiing, and one of the world's highest cable cars.

Pahalgam

Riverside meadows, and base for the Amarnath Yatra.

Sonmarg

Meadow of Gold, and the gateway to Ladakh.

Jammu

City of Temples, and gateway to Vaishno Devi.
View All Kashmir → Full region overview

Region 4 of 7

Uttarakhand

Lake-side hill stations, the Ganges at Rishikesh, and skiing under Nanda Devi.

Nainital

Boating on Naini Lake, and a proper hill-station Mall Road.

Mussoorie

The "Queen of the Hills" — waterfalls and two cable cars.

Rishikesh

On the Ganges — yoga, rafting, and Char Dham's start.

Dehradun

The valley gateway to most of the region's hill stations.

Auli

Skiing under Nanda Devi, and a long cable car.
View All Uttarakhand → Full region overview

Region 5 of 7

Sikkim

Kangchenjunga on the skyline, and a border pass that once carried the Silk Route.

Gangtok

The capital — MG Marg, and Rumtek Monastery.

Pelling

The best straight-on views of Kangchenjunga.

Lachung

The base village for Yumthang and Zero Point.

Yumthang Valley

Sikkim's Valley of Flowers, and hot springs.

Nathula

The old Silk Route pass, on the India–China border.
View All Sikkim → Full region overview

Region 6 of 7

Delhi

The gateway most Himalaya-bound trips pass through first.

Connaught Place

The colonial-era circular plaza and commercial heart.

Chandni Chowk

Shah Jahan's historic market street, and Jama Masjid.

India Gate

A 42m memorial, and the city's best evening lawns.

Qutub Minar

A 73m minaret begun in 1193, and the Iron Pillar.

Humayun's Tomb

Mughal gardens that inspired the Taj Mahal.
View All Delhi → Full region overview

Region 7 of 7

Chandigarh

India's first planned city, and the last stop before Himachal on most routes north.

Sector 17

The city's central pedestrian shopping plaza.

Rock Garden

Nek Chand's sculpture garden, built from industrial waste.

Sukhna Lake

A 1958 lake, and the city's morning-walk promenade.

Rose Garden

One of Asia's largest rose gardens.

Capitol Complex

Le Corbusier's UNESCO-listed government complex.
