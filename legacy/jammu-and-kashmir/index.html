Jammu & Kashmir

Houseboats on Dal Lake, ski slopes at Gulmarg, and the pilgrim road to Vaishno Devi — the valley that gave the Himalaya its reputation for beauty.

5Places live: Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam, Sonmarg, Jammu
Year-RoundSki season in winter, gardens and meadows in summer
25Years operating across Northern India

Five places, from the lake to the passes.

Kashmir earns its reputation honestly — and Jammu carries a different, older kind of pilgrimage tradition. Pick a place, or tell us the shape of the trip you're after.

Srinagar

Summer capital

Dal Lake houseboats and Mughal gardens — the valley's heart.

Gulmarg

~2,650m

Skiing, and one of the world's highest cable cars.

Pahalgam

Valley of Shepherds

Riverside meadows, and base for the Amarnath Yatra.

Sonmarg

~2,800m

Meadow of Gold, and the gateway to Ladakh via Zoji La.

Jammu

Winter capital

City of Temples, and gateway to Vaishno Devi.

Seasons matter more here than almost anywhere else we operate.

Kashmir isn't one trip — it's several, depending entirely on when you go. Spring is tulips and gardens, winter is skiing at Gulmarg, and the Amarnath Yatra season shapes travel through Pahalgam every summer. We route around whichever version of the valley you're actually after.

Jammu runs on its own separate rhythm as a year-round pilgrimage base, particularly for Vaishno Devi — we treat it as its own leg rather than an afterthought.
