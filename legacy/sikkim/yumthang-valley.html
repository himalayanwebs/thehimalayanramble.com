Yumthang Valley

~3,564m · Sikkim's "Valley of Flowers" — a rhododendron sanctuary with hot springs and a road that keeps climbing toward Tibet.

Plan Your Yumthang Trip

A valley that blooms on a schedule.

Yumthang is a designated rhododendron sanctuary, and when the flowers peak — usually March through April — the valley floor turns into a wide sweep of colour against snow-streaked peaks. Even outside bloom season, the valley's hot springs and the sheer scale of the landscape make it worth the drive from Lachung.

For travelers with the right permits, the road continues to Zero Point, near 4,600m — snow-capped even in summer, and close enough to Tibet to feel it.

Flowers, hot springs, and one further point.

Rhododendron Sanctuary

Dozens of rhododendron species bloom through the valley, peaking March–April.

Yumthang Hot Springs

Natural hot springs on the valley floor, a welcome stop after the cold drive in.

Zero Point

A further permit-controlled point near 4,600m, close to the Tibet border and snow-covered most of the year.

Good To Know

This is a day trip from Lachung, not an overnight stop — the valley has no permanent accommodation.
