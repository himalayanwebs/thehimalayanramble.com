Gulmarg

~2,650m · the "Meadow of Flowers," and Kashmir's main ski destination.

Plan Your Gulmarg Trip

A meadow in summer, a ski resort in winter.

Gulmarg's name means exactly what it sounds like — a meadow of flowers, and in the warmer months it lives up to it, rolling grassland framed by pine forest. But it's winter that Gulmarg is really known for: it's Kashmir's premier ski resort, with slopes and powder that draw skiers from across the country and beyond.

The Gulmarg Gondola, one of the highest cable cars in the world, runs in two stages up to Apharwat Peak at nearly 3,980m — open to both skiers and anyone who just wants the view.

Best time & getting here.

Best Time to Visit

December–February for skiing. April–June for meadows without the snow crowds.

Getting Here

About an hour and a half's drive from Srinagar — usually run as a day trip or short overnight from there.

Activities

Skiing and snowboarding in winter, gondola rides year-round, golf and trekking in summer.

Good To Know

Ski equipment and instructors are available to hire locally — no need to bring your own gear unless you prefer to.
